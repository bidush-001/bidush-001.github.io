Project Overview

This project developed an interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing customer reviews of British Airways. The dashboard visualizes ratings across service dimensions, such as cabin staff, seat comfort, food, entertainment, and overall flight experience, while providing geographic and travel-class insights.

Dashboard Features

  • Trend Analysis: Shows how overall customer satisfaction varies over time
  • Global Mapping: Displays average ratings by passenger origin country for regional insights
  • Service Breakdown: Interactive bar charts and filters let users explore key service aspects (e.g., seat comfort, staff performance) by route, travel class, and traveler type

Business Insights

  • Identified recurring low-rated service areas (e.g., in-flight comfort or food quality)
  • Tracked seasonal and route-specific trends in customer satisfaction
  • Enabled targeted improvement strategies for operations and service teams
